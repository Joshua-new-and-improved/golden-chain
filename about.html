About Golden Chain

Last updated 12 November 2026

Golden Chain is a free Catholic Bible and study web app at https://joshua-new-and-improved.github.io/golden-chain/. It is named after the Catena Aurea ("Golden Chain"), St Thomas Aquinas's commentary on the Gospels built from the Church Fathers.

What the app offers (free)

  • The whole Catholic Bible, with commentary from the Church Fathers and St Thomas Aquinas.
  • The daily Mass readings with short explanations, the Liturgy of the Hours, prayers and a liturgical calendar.
  • The Catechism, a theology "Academy" of structured lessons, apologetics, saints' lives, a glossary and flashcards.
  • Groups: free accounts that let friends, parishes and Bible studies pray and read together.

There are no adverts, no paid tiers and no paywall. Nothing in the app is sold.

What payments are for: voluntary donations

The only payments Golden Chain accepts are voluntary donations from people who want to help keep the app free. A donation can be:

  • Once-off: suggested amounts R50, R100, R250 or R500, or any amount the donor chooses.
  • Monthly: suggested amounts R30, R50, R100 or R250 a month, charged to a card until the donor stops it.

Donations cover running costs (web hosting, the database behind Groups, the domain) and the time spent writing and checking content. A donation does not buy any product, service, content or access: everything in the app is free whether or not someone donates. Donors receive a thank-you, and if they are signed in to Groups, an optional heart badge and an optional first-name mention on a thank-you wall.

No physical goods, branded goods or high-value items are sold, so nothing is shipped. Payments are processed by PayFast; Golden Chain never sees or stores card or bank details.
