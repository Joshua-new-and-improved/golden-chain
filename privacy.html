Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Golden Chain collects and how it is used, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). The responsible party is Joshua van den Heever (contact details below).

1. Using the app without an account

You can read, pray and study without an account. Your progress, highlights, notes and settings are stored only in your own browser (local storage) and are not sent to us. The app does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

2. Groups accounts

If you create a Groups account we store your email address, a display name, and what you post or do in groups (messages, prayer requests, RSVPs, reading progress you choose to share). This is used only to run Groups. It is stored with our database provider, Supabase, whose servers may be outside South Africa; we rely on the provider's security safeguards. Other members of a group can see what you post in that group.

3. Donations

When you donate, PayFast collects your payment details on its own secure pages under its own privacy policy. We receive and keep a record of the donation (amount, once-off or monthly, date, status, PayFast reference, and your name and email as given to PayFast) so we can confirm it, let you stop a monthly gift and answer queries. If you choose, your first name appears on the app's thank-you wall. We never see or store card or bank details.

4. Hosting

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information (such as IP addresses) for security, as any web host does.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information or share it for marketing. We share it only with the service providers above (Supabase, PayFast, GitHub) to run the app, or when the law requires.

6. Keeping and deleting

Account data is kept while your account exists. Email us at any time to have your account and information deleted; we do this within 30 days. Donation records are kept for as long as needed for accounting and legal purposes.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, and object to its use. Email vandenheeverjandre@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za.

8. Children

Groups is meant for people aged 13 and over; younger users need a parent's or guardian's permission.
