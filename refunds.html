Donations, refunds and delivery policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

This policy covers the voluntary donations Golden Chain accepts through PayFast.

1. What a donation is

Golden Chain sells nothing. The app is free for everyone. A donation is a voluntary gift toward the app's running costs; it does not buy goods, services, content or access. Amounts are in South African rand (ZAR).

2. Delivery and shipping

No physical or digital goods are supplied, so there is nothing to ship or deliver. After a successful payment you are shown a thank-you page in the app, and PayFast emails you a receipt. Nothing else is posted or sent.

3. Monthly donations: stopping them

You can stop a monthly donation at any time, with no fee or notice period:

  • in the app, under Support → "Your giving" → Stop (if you donated while signed in to Groups);
  • through the link in PayFast's confirmation email; or
  • by emailing vandenheeverjandre@gmail.com, and we will cancel it for you within 2 working days.

Stopping prevents future charges. Payments already taken before you stopped are covered by section 4.

4. Refunds

Because donations are gifts, they are generally not refundable. We will refund a donation in full if:

  • it was made by mistake, for example the wrong amount, a duplicate payment, or a monthly donation charged after you asked us to stop it;
  • your card or account was used without your permission; or
  • a refund is otherwise required by law.

To ask for a refund, email vandenheeverjandre@gmail.com within 30 days of the payment with the date, amount and the PayFast payment reference from your receipt. We reply within 5 working days. Approved refunds are made through PayFast to the original payment method; depending on your bank, they can take up to 10 working days to show.

5. Security

Payments are processed by PayFast, a PCI DSS compliant payment provider. Golden Chain never sees or stores your card or bank details.
