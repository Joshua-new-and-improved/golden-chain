psalms 51|The boastful man|Why boast of evil, you who love lies? God will uproot you. I am like a green olive tree in God's house.|The slanderer's power is brief.
psalms 52|No one who does good|The fool says there is no God. (Almost the same as Psalm 13.)|Without God, people devour one another.
psalms 53|Save me by your name|Betrayed to Saul, David prays, "God is my helper."|A short prayer for rescue.
psalms 54|Betrayed by a friend|"Oh that I had wings like a dove!" It was not an enemy but my companion.|"Cast your care upon the Lord, and he will sustain you."
psalms 55|In God I trust|"When I am afraid, I put my trust in you." You have counted my tears.|God keeps a record of every sorrow.
psalms 56|In the shadow of your wings|Hiding in a cave from Saul, David sings, "My heart is ready. Awake, my soul!"|Praise in the middle of danger.
psalms 57|Against unjust judges|Do you rulers judge rightly? They are deaf like a snake that will not hear the charmer.|"Truly there is a God who judges on earth."
psalms 58|God is my fortress|Enemies prowl like dogs around the city at night. "I will sing of your strength in the morning."|Confidence while surrounded.
psalms 59|After a defeat|"You have rejected us." Give us help against the enemy.|"Vain is the help of man."
psalms 60|Lead me to the rock|"From the ends of the earth I cry to you. Lead me to the rock that is higher than I."|God is a tower of strength.
psalms 61|In God alone|"My soul rests in God alone." If riches increase, do not set your heart on them.|Only God is a solid foundation.
psalms 62|My soul thirsts for you|"O God, you are my God; at dawn I seek you. Your love is better than life."|The classic morning psalm of longing for God.
psalms 63|Hidden plots|Enemies sharpen their tongues like swords. God shoots his own arrow.|God turns slander back on itself.
psalms 64|A harvest hymn|"To you all flesh will come." You visit the earth and water it; the valleys shout for joy.|Thanks for forgiveness and for the fruits of the earth.
psalms 65|Come and see|"Shout with joy to God, all the earth." He tested us like silver and brought us through fire and water.|Praise after a trial.
psalms 66|Let the peoples praise you|"May God be gracious to us and bless us, that your way be known on earth."|A missionary prayer: blessing is meant to spread.
psalms 67|Let God arise|A triumphal procession: God, father of orphans and defender of widows, marches before his people.|Paul applies "he ascended on high" to Christ.
psalms 68|The waters have reached my neck|"I am sinking in deep mire." Zeal for your house consumes me. They gave me vinegar to drink.|The Gospels see this fulfilled in the Passion.
psalms 69|O God, come to my aid|"O Lord, make haste to help me."|These words open each hour of the Divine Office.
psalms 70|Do not cast me off in old age|"You have been my hope from my youth." Even when I am old and grey, do not forsake me.|A prayer for the elderly.
psalms 71|The king of justice and peace|"May he defend the poor. May he rule from sea to sea." Kings will bring him gifts.|Read on the Epiphany of Christ.
psalms 72|When the wicked prosper|"My feet had almost slipped," envying the arrogant. "Until I went into the sanctuary of God." Whom have I in heaven but you?|The answer to envy is God himself.
psalms 73|The sanctuary destroyed|Enemies have smashed and burned the Temple. "Why, O God, have you cast us off?"|A prayer for the Church in times of ruin.
psalms 74|God is the judge|"He brings one down and lifts up another."|Pride meets its judge.
psalms 75|God the awesome defender|"In Judah God is known." He broke the weapons of war.|Even human anger ends up serving God's praise.
psalms 76|Has God forgotten to be gracious?|In the night I cannot sleep. Then: "I will remember the works of the Lord."|Memory of God's deeds heals despair.
psalms 77|Lessons from history|A long retelling from Egypt to David: God's gifts, the people's ingratitude, his patience.|Tell the next generation, so they do not repeat it.
psalms 78|Jerusalem in ruins|The nations have defiled your temple. "Do not remember our former sins. Help us, for the glory of your name."|A prayer from catastrophe.
psalms 79|Restore us, O God|"Shepherd of Israel, hear us." You brought a vine out of Egypt; why are its walls broken?|"Let your face shine, and we shall be saved."
psalms 80|If only my people would listen|A festival song. "Open your mouth wide and I will fill it." But my people did not listen.|God longs to feed his people with the finest wheat.
psalms 81|God judges the judges|"How long will you judge unjustly? Defend the poor and the orphan."|Jesus quotes "You are gods" in John 10.
psalms 82|Enemies conspire|Nations plot to wipe out Israel.|That they may know that you alone are Most High.
psalms 83|How lovely is your dwelling place|"My soul longs for the courts of the Lord. One day in your courts is better than a thousand elsewhere."|The joy of being in God's house.
psalms 84|Mercy and truth have met|"Lord, you have blessed your land." Justice and peace have kissed.|An Advent psalm of salvation near at hand.
psalms 85|Teach me your way|"Incline your ear, for I am poor and needy. You, Lord, are good and forgiving."|A humble personal prayer.
psalms 86|Zion, mother of all|Glorious things are said of the city of God. Of every nation it will be said, "This one was born there."|The Church as mother of all peoples.
psalms 87|The darkest psalm|"My soul is full of troubles." You have taken friend and neighbour from me.|The only psalm with no turn to hope, yet it is still prayed to God.
psalms 88|God's promise to David|"I will sing of the mercies of the Lord for ever." God swore an everlasting throne to David. But now the king is cast off: where is your former love?|Holding God to his promise in the dark.
psalms 89|Teach us to number our days|"Lord, you have been our refuge. A thousand years in your sight are like yesterday."|Attributed to Moses: human life is short, God eternal.
psalms 90|Under God's protection|"He will give his angels charge over you. You shall not fear the terror of the night."|Prayed at Night Prayer. The devil quotes it to Jesus. (Psalm 91 in modern Bibles.)
psalms 91|It is good to give thanks|A psalm for the Sabbath. The just flourish like the palm tree and still bear fruit in old age.|Praise morning and night.
psalms 92|The Lord is king|"He is robed in majesty." Mightier than the thunder of great waters is the Lord.|Creation is firm because God reigns.
psalms 93|God of justice|"How long shall the wicked triumph?" Does he who made the ear not hear?|God sees.
psalms 94|Come, let us sing|"Let us kneel before the Lord who made us. Today, if you hear his voice, harden not your hearts."|Opens the Church's daily prayer.
psalms 95|Sing a new song|"Tell his glory among the nations." Let the heavens rejoice.|The Lord comes to judge the earth.
psalms 96|The Lord reigns|Clouds and fire surround him; the mountains melt like wax.|"Light dawns for the just."
psalms 97|All the ends of the earth|"Sing a new song, for he has done wonderful things." Let the rivers clap their hands.|Sung at Christmas.
psalms 98|Holy is he|The Lord reigns, enthroned on the cherubim. Moses, Aaron and Samuel called on him.|God is holy and forgiving.
psalms 99|Serve the Lord with gladness|"Sing joyfully to God, all the earth. We are his people, the sheep of his pasture."|A short psalm of pure thanks.
psalms 100|A ruler's resolutions|"I will walk with blameless heart within my house."|A rule of life for anyone in authority.
