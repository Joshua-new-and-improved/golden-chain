psalms 101|Prayer of the afflicted|"My days vanish like smoke." But you, Lord, remain for ever.|The fifth penitential psalm.
psalms 102|Bless the Lord, my soul|"He forgives all your sins. As far as east is from west, so far he removes our sins. As a father pities his children."|The great psalm of God's mercy. (Psalm 103 in modern Bibles.)
psalms 103|Hymn to the Creator|God stretches out the heavens, waters the hills, feeds every creature. "Send forth your Spirit, and they are created."|Sung at Pentecost.
psalms 104|God's faithfulness in history|From Abraham through Joseph to the Exodus: "He remembered his covenant for ever."|Thanksgiving by remembering.
psalms 105|Israel's unfaithfulness|The same history from the other side: "We have sinned like our fathers." Yet he heard their cry.|Confession joined to trust.
psalms 106|Let the redeemed say so|Four groups cry to the Lord: the lost, prisoners, the sick and sailors in a storm. He saves each.|"Give thanks to the Lord for his mercy."
psalms 107|My heart is ready|"I will awake the dawn." Then a prayer for victory.|Praise leads into petition.
psalms 108|A prayer against slanderers|"In return for my love they accuse me." Strong curses follow.|A hard psalm. Acts applies part of it to Judas.
psalms 109|The Lord said to my Lord|"Sit at my right hand. You are a priest for ever, in the order of Melchizedek."|The psalm most quoted in the New Testament, about Christ as King and Priest. (Psalm 110 in modern Bibles.)
psalms 110|Great are the works of the Lord|"He has given food to those who fear him. He sent redemption to his people."|The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom.
psalms 111|The happiness of the just|He is generous and lends, and his heart is steady.|"A light rises in the darkness for the upright."
psalms 112|He raises the poor from the dust|"From the rising of the sun to its setting, praised be the name of the Lord."|God stoops down to the lowly.
psalms 113|When Israel came out of Egypt|The sea fled and the mountains skipped. Idols have mouths and do not speak. "Not to us, Lord, but to your name give glory."|An Easter psalm.
psalms 114|I love the Lord|"He heard my voice. The cords of death surrounded me."|"I will walk before the Lord in the land of the living."
psalms 115|What shall I render to the Lord?|"I will take the chalice of salvation. Precious in the eyes of the Lord is the death of his saints."|Prayed in connection with the Eucharist.
psalms 116|The shortest psalm|"Praise the Lord, all nations."|Two verses that include the whole world.
psalms 117|The stone the builders rejected|"Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good." The rejected stone has become the cornerstone. "This is the day the Lord has made."|The great Easter psalm.
psalms 118|In praise of God's law|The longest psalm, 176 verses, each one about God's word. "Your word is a lamp to my feet."|A long meditation on loving God's will. (Psalm 119 in modern Bibles.)
psalms 119|A pilgrim among liars|"In my trouble I cried to the Lord." I am for peace; they are for war.|The first of fifteen pilgrim songs for going up to Jerusalem.
psalms 120|I lift up my eyes to the mountains|"My help comes from the Lord. He who keeps you will not sleep."|A traveller's psalm.
psalms 121|Let us go to the house of the Lord|"I rejoiced when they said to me." Pray for the peace of Jerusalem.|The joy of arriving for worship.
psalms 122|Eyes on the Lord|As servants watch their master's hand, so our eyes are on the Lord until he has mercy.|Patient waiting.
psalms 123|If the Lord had not been with us|We would have been swallowed alive. "Our soul has escaped like a bird from the snare."|"Our help is in the name of the Lord."
psalms 124|Like Mount Zion|Those who trust in the Lord cannot be shaken. As mountains surround Jerusalem, the Lord surrounds his people.|Security in God.
psalms 125|Those who sow in tears|"When the Lord brought back the captives, we were like men dreaming." They shall reap with joy.|Sorrow now, harvest later.
psalms 126|Unless the Lord builds the house|The builders labour in vain. Children are a gift from the Lord.|Our effort needs God's blessing.
psalms 127|The blessing of family|"Blessed are all who fear the Lord." Your wife like a fruitful vine, your children like olive shoots around your table.|Sung at weddings.
psalms 128|They have not prevailed|Often attacked since my youth, yet not overcome.|God's people outlast their persecutors.
psalms 129|Out of the depths|"If you, Lord, mark iniquities, who shall stand? But with you is forgiveness. My soul waits for the Lord."|The De profundis, prayed for the dead. (Psalm 130 in modern Bibles.)
psalms 130|Like a weaned child|"My heart is not proud." I have quieted my soul like a child with its mother.|Humble trust.
psalms 131|David's vow|David swore not to rest until he found a place for the Lord. God swore that his descendant would sit on the throne.|Read of Christ and of Mary, the new ark.
psalms 132|Brothers in unity|"How good and pleasant it is when brothers live together in unity."|Unity is a blessing, like precious oil.
psalms 133|Night praise|"Bless the Lord, all you servants of the Lord, who stand by night in his house."|The last pilgrim song.
psalms 134|Praise the name of the Lord|He does whatever he pleases in heaven and on earth. He struck Egypt. Idols are silver and gold.|God alone is worth praising.
psalms 135|His mercy endures for ever|Creation, the Exodus and the gift of the land, each line answered, "for his mercy endures for ever".|A litany of thanks.
psalms 136|By the rivers of Babylon|"We sat and wept when we remembered Zion. How shall we sing the Lord's song in a foreign land?"|The exile's grief and loyalty. Its last verse is a cry of raw pain.
psalms 137|Thanks with my whole heart|"On the day I called, you answered me."|"Do not forsake the work of your hands."
psalms 138|Lord, you have searched me|"You know when I sit and when I rise. Where can I flee from your presence? You knit me together in my mother's womb."|God knows us completely and made us with care.
psalms 139|Deliver me from the evil man|They sharpen their tongues like serpents.|"The Lord will maintain the cause of the poor."
psalms 140|Like incense|"Let my prayer rise before you like incense. Set a guard over my mouth."|An evening prayer.
psalms 141|In the cave|"I cry to the Lord with my voice. No one cares for my soul. You are my refuge."|Francis of Assisi prayed this as he died.
psalms 142|Teach me to do your will|"Do not enter into judgement with your servant. I stretch out my hands to you."|The seventh penitential psalm.
psalms 143|Blessed be the Lord, my rock|"Lord, what is man that you care for him?"|"Happy the people whose God is the Lord."
psalms 144|Great is the Lord|"The Lord is gracious and merciful. You open your hand and satisfy every living thing."|Used as grace before meals.
psalms 145|Put no trust in princes|God keeps faith for ever: he feeds the hungry, frees prisoners, protects the stranger.|Trust God, not the powerful.
psalms 146|He heals the broken-hearted|He builds up Jerusalem and counts the stars by name.|The God of the galaxies bends over the wounded.
psalms 147|Praise the Lord, Jerusalem|He blesses your children, gives peace, sends his word like snow.|Thanks for God's word, given to his people.
psalms 148|All creation, praise|Angels, sun, moon, sea monsters, mountains, kings and children: praise the Lord.|Everything that exists is called to worship.
psalms 149|A new song|"Let Israel rejoice in its Maker. The Lord takes delight in his people."|Praise with dancing.
psalms 150|Let everything that breathes praise the Lord|Praise him with trumpet, harp, strings, pipes and cymbals.|The Psalter ends in pure praise.
