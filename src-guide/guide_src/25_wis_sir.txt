wisdom 1|Love justice|Rulers are told to seek God with a sincere heart. Wisdom will not enter a deceitful soul. God did not make death and takes no pleasure in the destruction of the living.|Death entered through sin; God made us for life.
wisdom 2|The reasoning of the wicked|The godless say life is short and ends in nothing, so enjoy it and crush the weak. They plot against the just man who calls God his father: "Let us condemn him to a shameful death."|The Church reads this as a prophecy of Christ's Passion.
wisdom 3|The souls of the just|"The souls of the just are in the hand of God, and no torment shall touch them." They seemed to die, but they are at peace.|Read at funerals. Death is not the end for those who are God's.
wisdom 4|An early death|Better childless with virtue than many wicked children. The just man who dies young is at rest: he was taken so that evil would not corrupt him.|A long life is not the measure of a good one.
wisdom 5|The wicked see their mistake|At the judgement the wicked will see the just man they mocked and say, "We fools! How he is numbered among the sons of God!"|Everything will look different at the end.
wisdom 6|Seek wisdom, you kings|Power is given by God, and the mighty will be strictly examined. Wisdom is easily found by those who love her.|Authority brings stricter judgement.
wisdom 7|Solomon prays for wisdom|Solomon says he was born like everyone else. He prayed, and wisdom came. She is a breath of God's power, a reflection of eternal light.|The New Testament uses this language for Christ.
wisdom 8|Wisdom as a bride|Solomon loved wisdom and sought her as his bride. She teaches temperance, prudence, justice and fortitude.|The four cardinal virtues are named here.
wisdom 9|Solomon's prayer|"God of my fathers, give me the wisdom that sits by your throne." Who can know God's counsel unless he sends his Spirit from on high?|A model prayer for anyone with responsibility.
wisdom 10|Wisdom in history|Wisdom protected Adam, Noah, Abraham, Lot, Jacob and Joseph, and led Israel out of Egypt.|God's wisdom has guided the whole story.
wisdom 11|Water from the rock|Israel was given water in the desert while Egypt's river turned to blood. "You love all things that exist; you spare all, because they are yours, O Lord, lover of souls."|God hates nothing he has made.
wisdom 12|God's patience|God judged even the Canaanites little by little, giving time to repent. "You have taught your people that the just must be kind."|God's power is shown in mercy.
wisdom 13|The folly of worshipping nature|People admired fire, wind and stars and took them for gods, when from the greatness and beauty of created things their Maker can be known.|A key text for knowing God by reason from creation.
wisdom 14|How idolatry began|A man prays to a piece of wood for a safe voyage. Idols began with grief and flattery and led to every kind of moral disorder.|False worship corrupts life.
wisdom 15|We are yours|"Even if we sin, we are yours." The potter makes a god from the same clay as a pot.|To know God is the root of immortality.
wisdom 16|Plagues and mercies|Egypt was tormented by creatures, while Israel received quail and manna, "bread from heaven, containing every delight".|The Church uses this line of the Eucharist.
wisdom 17|Darkness over Egypt|The Egyptians, prisoners of darkness, were terrified by their own guilty consciences.|"Wickedness is a cowardly thing, condemned by its own witness."
wisdom 18|The night of the Passover|Israel had light. "While gentle silence enveloped all things, your all-powerful Word leaped from heaven."|The liturgy applies this to Christmas night.
wisdom 19|Through the Red Sea|Creation itself was refashioned to serve God's children as they crossed the sea.|"In all things, O Lord, you have exalted your people."
sirach 1|All wisdom is from the Lord|Wisdom was created before all things, and God poured her out on those who love him. The fear of the Lord is the beginning, fullness and crown of wisdom.|Reverence for God is the root of a wise life.
sirach 2|Prepare for testing|"My son, when you come to serve the Lord, prepare yourself for trials." Gold is tested in fire. Has anyone trusted in the Lord and been disappointed?|Serving God includes being tested.
sirach 3|Honour your parents|Whoever honours his father atones for sins. Help your father in his old age, even if his mind fails. Be humble.|The fourth commandment spelled out with tenderness.
sirach 4|Do not turn from the poor|Do not make the needy wait. Wisdom tests her children at first, then gladdens them. Fight to the death for truth.|Charity, and courage for the truth.
sirach 5|Do not presume on mercy|Do not say, "His mercy is great; he will forgive." Do not delay turning to the Lord. Be quick to hear and slow to answer.|Presuming on forgiveness is a trap.
sirach 6|Friendship|"A faithful friend is a sturdy shelter; whoever finds one has found a treasure." Test a friend before trusting.|Real friends are rare and beyond price.
sirach 7|Various counsels|Do no evil. Do not seek high office. Honour your priest, stretch out your hand to the poor, visit the sick. "In all you do, remember the end of your life."|Remembering death keeps us from sin.
sirach 8|Prudence with others|Do not quarrel with the powerful or the hot-tempered. Do not despise the old, for we too will grow old.|Learn from the elderly.
sirach 9|Women and friends|Guard your eyes and heart. Do not abandon an old friend. Do not envy a sinner's success.|Purity and loyalty.
sirach 10|Pride|A wise ruler makes a stable people. "Pride is the beginning of all sin." Why are dust and ashes proud?|Pride is the root sin.
sirach 11|Do not judge by appearances|The bee is small, but her produce is sweet. Do not find fault before you investigate. Call no one happy before his death.|Only the end shows what a life was.
sirach 12|Care in doing good; false friends|Never trust an enemy; his malice corrodes like rust.|Prudence about whom to trust.
sirach 13|Rich and poor|"Whoever touches pitch gets dirty." The rich use the poor and drop them.|Choose your company carefully.
sirach 14|Use your goods well|Blessed is the one who has not sinned with his tongue. A miser's wealth is no use to him. Do good before you die.|Generosity now; you cannot take it with you.
sirach 15|Free will|Do not say, "God made me sin." He set fire and water before you: stretch out your hand to whichever you choose.|A clear statement of human freedom and responsibility.
sirach 16|God sees everything|Do not rejoice in many godless children. Do not say, "I am hidden from the Lord."|No one is lost in the crowd before God.
sirach 17|Man made in God's image|God gave people tongue, eyes, ears and a mind, and made a covenant with them. "How great is the mercy of the Lord to those who turn to him."|Our dignity and God's mercy.
sirach 18|God's greatness and patience|A human life is a drop in the sea, so God is patient. A kind word is better than a gift. Before falling ill, humble yourself.|God's mercy reaches all flesh.
sirach 19|Guard your tongue|Whoever despises small things falls little by little. "Have you heard something? Let it die with you." Question a friend before believing a rumour.|Do not pass on gossip.
sirach 20|Silence and speech|Some keep silent and are wise. A slip on the pavement is better than a slip of the tongue.|Know when to speak.
sirach 21|Flee from sin|"Have you sinned? Do so no more, and pray about your former sins." Flee from sin as from a snake.|Repent, and do not go back.
sirach 22|The lazy and the fool|Mourn for a fool more than for the dead. Remain loyal to a friend in his poverty.|"Who will set a guard over my mouth?"
sirach 23|A prayer for self-control|"Lord, Father and Ruler of my life," keep me from proud eyes and evil desire. Warnings against swearing and adultery.|Ask God for help with speech and purity.
sirach 24|Wisdom praises herself|Wisdom came from the mouth of the Most High and made her home in Israel. "I am the mother of fair love." She is the book of the covenant.|The Church applies this chapter to Our Lady.
sirach 25|Things beautiful and hateful|Harmony among brothers, friendship among neighbours, and a wife and husband who live as one are beautiful. The crown of the old is experience.|A good marriage is one of life's best gifts.
sirach 26|A good wife|"Happy the husband of a good wife." Her beauty in a well-kept home is like the sun rising.|A warm portrait of a good spouse.
sirach 27|Tested by conversation|As the kiln tests the potter's vessels, a person is tested by how he talks. Whoever betrays a secret destroys trust.|Speech shows character.
sirach 28|Forgive your neighbour|"Forgive your neighbour's wrong, and then your sins will be pardoned when you pray." The blow of the tongue breaks bones.|The same teaching Jesus gives in the Our Father.
sirach 29|Lending and giving|Lend to your neighbour in need, and repay on time. Store up almsgiving in your treasury.|Mercy with money.
sirach 30|Raising children; health|A father who loves his son corrects him. Health is better than any gold. Do not give yourself to sorrow.|Discipline, health and a cheerful heart.
sirach 31|Riches and table manners|Sleeplessness over wealth wastes the flesh. Blessed the rich man found blameless. Wine in moderation is life.|Moderation in money, food and drink.
sirach 32|At a banquet|If you are made master of the feast, do not be puffed up. Speak briefly. Do nothing without counsel.|Good manners are part of wisdom.
sirach 33|Trust and order|No evil will befall the one who fears the Lord. God made days and people different, like clay in the potter's hand.|Keep control of your own affairs while you live.
sirach 34|Dreams and true worship|Dreams are empty hopes. Offering sacrifice from what was stolen from the poor is like killing a son before his father's eyes.|God does not accept worship paid for by injustice.
sirach 35|The prayer of the humble|Keeping the law is itself an offering. "The prayer of the humble pierces the clouds."|God hears the poor and the wronged.
sirach 36|A prayer for Israel|"Have mercy on us, O God of all." Gather the tribes of Jacob. Then sayings on choosing a wife.|A prayer for God's people.
sirach 37|Advisers|Some friends are friends in name only. Seek advice from a godly person, and above all pray to the Most High.|Choose advisers who fear God.
sirach 38|The physician|"Honour the physician, for the Lord created him." Pray, and also call the doctor. Mourn the dead, then be comforted. Craftsmen keep the world going.|Faith and medicine work together.
sirach 39|The scribe|The one who studies the law of the Most High will pour out wisdom. "All the works of the Lord are very good."|Study of Scripture is a calling.
sirach 40|The hard lot of man|Much labour is created for everyone, from birth to burial. But the fear of the Lord is like a garden of blessing.|Life is hard; reverence for God is its shelter.
sirach 41|Death and a good name|"O death, how bitter is the thought of you" to one at peace with his possessions. A good name lasts for ever.|Death is feared most by those most attached.
sirach 42|The works of God|What to be ashamed of and what not. Then praise: God has ordered all things, and nothing is made in vain.|Creation reflects God's wisdom.
sirach 43|Sun, moon and stars|A hymn to the splendour of sky, snow, storm and sea. "He is the all."|We can never praise God enough.
sirach 44|Let us now praise famous men|The praise of Israel's ancestors begins: Enoch, Noah, Abraham, Isaac and Jacob.|Holiness is remembered and held up as an example.
sirach 45|Moses and Aaron|Moses, beloved of God and men; Aaron and his priesthood; Phinehas.|God chose and equipped leaders for his people.
sirach 46|Joshua and Samuel|Joshua, Caleb, the judges and the prophet Samuel.|Faithful leaders in war and peace.
sirach 47|Nathan, David and Solomon|David praised God with his whole heart. Solomon was wise in youth and stained his honour later.|Even the great are judged honestly.
sirach 48|Elijah and Elisha|Elijah arose like fire. Elisha was filled with his spirit. Hezekiah and Isaiah.|The prophets kept Israel alive.
sirach 49|Josiah and the later prophets|Josiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, the twelve prophets, Zerubbabel, Nehemiah.|The line of the faithful continues to the author's day.
sirach 50|Simon the high priest|A splendid description of the high priest Simon leading the Temple liturgy, like the morning star among clouds.|A picture of Israel's worship at its best.
sirach 51|A prayer of thanks|The author thanks God for rescuing him from death and tells how he sought wisdom from his youth. "Come to me, you who are untaught."|The book ends in thanksgiving and an invitation.
