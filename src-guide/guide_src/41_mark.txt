mark 1|The Gospel begins at full speed|John the Baptist prepares the way. Jesus is baptised, tempted in the desert, and starts preaching: "The kingdom of God is at hand; repent and believe." He calls four fishermen, drives out a demon in the synagogue, heals Peter's mother-in-law and a leper.|Mark skips the birth stories and goes straight to action. From the first page Jesus acts with an authority no one has seen before.
mark 2|Authority to forgive sins|Four friends lower a paralysed man through a roof. Jesus forgives his sins, then heals him. He calls Levi the tax collector, eats with sinners, and defends his disciples over fasting and picking grain on the Sabbath.|"The Son of Man is Lord of the Sabbath." Jesus claims rights that belong to God alone.
mark 3|The Twelve chosen|Jesus heals a man's withered hand on the Sabbath, and his enemies begin plotting to kill him. He chooses twelve apostles. His relatives think he is out of his mind, and scribes say he is possessed. He says whoever does God's will is his brother, sister and mother.|People react to Jesus in opposite ways. Belonging to his family comes through obedience to God, not blood.
mark 4|Seeds and a storm|Jesus tells the parable of the sower and explains it, then the lamp, the seed that grows by itself, and the mustard seed. That evening a storm hits the boat while he sleeps. He wakes, tells the wind and sea to be still, and they obey.|God's word grows in hearts that receive it. The disciples ask the right question: "Who then is this?"
mark 5|Three people beyond human help|Jesus frees a man possessed by a "legion" of demons, who rush into a herd of pigs. A woman ill for twelve years touches his cloak and is healed. He takes a dead twelve-year-old girl by the hand and says, "Little girl, arise."|Demons, disease and death all give way to him. Faith, even shaky faith, is what reaches him.
mark 6|Rejected at home; loaves and fish|Nazareth takes offence at Jesus. He sends the Twelve out two by two. Herod has John the Baptist beheaded at a banquet. Jesus feeds five thousand and walks on the sea.|A prophet is not honoured in his own town. The Shepherd feeds his people when they have nothing.
mark 7|Clean hands and clean hearts|Pharisees complain that the disciples eat with unwashed hands. Jesus answers that evil comes from within, from the heart. He heals the daughter of a Syrophoenician woman and opens the ears of a deaf man, saying "Ephphatha", be opened.|Jesus reaches beyond Israel to Gentiles. The word Ephphatha is still used in the rite of baptism.
mark 8|"You are the Christ"|Jesus feeds four thousand and heals a blind man in two stages. At Caesarea Philippi Peter confesses that he is the Christ. Jesus at once speaks of suffering and death, and says anyone who follows him must take up his cross.|This is the turning point of Mark. The disciples see who Jesus is, but only partly, like the blind man at first.
mark 9|Glory on the mountain|Jesus is transfigured before Peter, James and John. He heals a boy after the father cries, "I believe; help my unbelief." He foretells his death again, and teaches that the greatest must be servant of all.|The father's prayer is one anyone can make. Greatness in the kingdom is measured by service.
mark 10|Marriage, riches and service|Jesus teaches that marriage is for life, welcomes children, and tells a rich man to sell what he has. James and John ask for seats of honour. Blind Bartimaeus cries out, is healed, and follows Jesus on the road.|"The Son of Man came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many."
mark 11|Jesus enters Jerusalem|Jesus rides in on a colt to shouts of "Hosanna". He curses a barren fig tree and clears the Temple of traders. The leaders ask by what authority he acts, and he answers with a question about John the Baptist.|The fig tree with leaves and no fruit is a picture of worship that looks alive and is not.
mark 12|Questions in the Temple|Jesus tells of tenants who kill the owner's son. He answers traps about taxes, the resurrection and the first commandment. Then he points out a poor widow who gives two small coins, which is all she has.|God measures a gift by the love and sacrifice in it, not by its size.
mark 13|Stay awake|Jesus foretells the destruction of the Temple, persecution of his followers, great distress, and his coming in glory. He gives no date. His command is repeated: watch.|Christians live expecting the Lord. That is meant to make us faithful, not frightened.
mark 14|The Passion begins|A woman pours costly perfume on Jesus. At the Last Supper he gives his Body and Blood. In Gethsemane he prays "Abba, Father" while the disciples sleep. He is betrayed with a kiss, condemned by the council, and denied by Peter.|Everyone abandons him. He walks into his Passion alone and willingly.
mark 15|The crucifixion|Pilate hands Jesus over to be crucified. Soldiers mock him with a crown of thorns. Simon of Cyrene carries the cross. Jesus dies with a loud cry, the Temple curtain is torn in two, and a Roman centurion says, "Truly this man was the Son of God."|The first person in Mark to confess Jesus as Son of God at the cross is a pagan soldier.
mark 16|The empty tomb|Women come to anoint the body and find the stone rolled away. A young man in white says, "He is risen; he is not here." Jesus appears to Mary Magdalene and others, sends the apostles into the whole world, and is taken up to heaven.|The apostles are sent to preach to every creature. The Church's mission starts from the empty tomb.
