1-maccabees 1|The persecution begins|After Alexander the Great, the Greek king Antiochus Epiphanes plunders the Temple, bans the Jewish law and sets up a pagan altar. Many give in; many choose death.|A culture demands that God's people abandon their faith.
1-maccabees 2|Mattathias|The old priest Mattathias refuses to offer pagan sacrifice and flees to the hills with his five sons. Dying, he tells them to give their lives for the covenant.|Resistance starts with one family saying no.
1-maccabees 3|Judas Maccabeus|Judas, "the Hammer", takes command and wins his first battles against much larger forces.|"Victory does not depend on numbers; strength comes from heaven."
1-maccabees 4|The Temple rededicated|Judas defeats two more armies, cleanses the Temple and relights its lamps. The feast of Dedication is established.|This is the origin of Hanukkah, which Jesus kept.
1-maccabees 5|Rescuing fellow Jews|Judas and his brothers fight to rescue Jews under attack in Galilee and Gilead.|Others who fight for their own glory are defeated.
1-maccabees 6|The death of Antiochus|Antiochus dies admitting the evil he did in Jerusalem. Eleazar dies killing a war elephant.|The persecutor ends in regret.
1-maccabees 7|Nicanor defeated|A new king sends the general Nicanor, who threatens the Temple. Judas defeats him.|Blasphemy is answered.
1-maccabees 8|Treaty with Rome|Judas hears of Rome's power and makes an alliance.|Politics enters the story.
1-maccabees 9|Judas falls|Judas dies in battle against overwhelming odds. His brother Jonathan takes his place.|"How is the mighty fallen, the saviour of Israel!"
1-maccabees 10|Jonathan high priest|Rival kings compete for Jonathan's support, and he becomes high priest.|He uses their rivalry wisely.
1-maccabees 11|Shifting alliances|Kings rise and fall, and Jonathan negotiates among them.|Loyalty among these rulers is short-lived.
1-maccabees 12|Jonathan captured|Jonathan renews ties with Rome and Sparta, then is tricked and seized by Trypho.|Trust placed in a liar.
1-maccabees 13|Simon and independence|Simon, the last brother, takes command. Jonathan is killed. "The yoke of the Gentiles was removed from Israel."|Freedom at last.
1-maccabees 14|In praise of Simon|The land has peace. The people record Simon's deeds on bronze tablets.|A portrait of peace under a good ruler.
1-maccabees 15|A new threat|King Antiochus VII first confirms Simon's rights, then turns on him.|Promises of the powerful shift with their interests.
1-maccabees 16|Simon murdered|Simon's sons defeat the enemy. Simon is killed at a banquet by his own son-in-law. His son John takes over.|The whole first generation gave their lives.
2-maccabees 1|Letters to Egypt|Jews in Jerusalem write to those in Egypt to keep the feast of the Temple's purification. A story of the sacred fire is told.|Jews everywhere share one worship.
2-maccabees 2|Jeremiah and the ark|Jeremiah is said to have hidden the ark in a cave. The author explains he is summarising a longer work.|The author is frank about how he wrote.
2-maccabees 3|Heliodorus|A royal official comes to seize the Temple treasury. A heavenly horseman and two young men strike him down.|God defends the holy place.
2-maccabees 4|The high priesthood for sale|Jason and then Menelaus buy the office with bribes and promote Greek ways. The good high priest Onias is murdered.|Corruption from inside comes before persecution from outside.
2-maccabees 5|The Temple plundered|Antiochus massacres the people of Jerusalem and loots the Temple.|God chose the place for the people's sake, not the people for the place.
2-maccabees 6|Eleazar|The law is banned. Ninety-year-old Eleazar refuses even to pretend to eat pork, so as not to mislead the young.|He will not give scandal to save his life.
2-maccabees 7|The mother and her seven sons|Seven brothers are tortured to death one by one, professing that God will raise them. Their mother encourages each and dies last.|A clear witness to the resurrection of the body and to creation from nothing.
2-maccabees 8|Judas's first victories|Judas gathers six thousand men and defeats Nicanor.|God's anger has turned to mercy.
2-maccabees 9|The end of Antiochus|Antiochus is struck with a horrible disease and dies in agony.|The proud persecutor learns too late.
2-maccabees 10|The Temple purified|Judas retakes the Temple and purifies it, and a yearly feast is decreed.|Worship is restored.
2-maccabees 11|Lysias defeated|A heavenly horseman leads Judas's army. Peace letters follow.|God fights for his people.
2-maccabees 12|Prayer for the dead|After a battle, the fallen are found wearing pagan charms. Judas takes a collection for a sacrifice for their sins.|A key text for praying for the dead and for the doctrine of purgatory.
2-maccabees 13|Another invasion|The young king invades. Menelaus is executed. Judas attacks by night, and a treaty is made.|The traitor meets his end.
2-maccabees 14|Nicanor and Razis|Nicanor is sent against Judas, befriends him, then threatens the Temple. The elder Razis dies defiantly.|A hard chapter from a time of terror.
2-maccabees 15|Judas's dream|Judas sees the dead high priest Onias and the prophet Jeremiah praying for the people. Nicanor is defeated.|Scriptural support for the intercession of the saints.
