james 1|Trials and doing the word|Count it joy when you are tested, because testing produces endurance. Ask God for wisdom without doubting. God tempts no one; every good gift comes from him. Be doers of the word and not hearers only.|Real religion is caring for orphans and widows and keeping oneself unstained by the world.
james 2|Faith without works is dead|Do not give the rich man the best seat and tell the poor man to stand. Whoever breaks one point of the law is guilty of all of it. What good is faith that does nothing for a brother who is cold and hungry? Abraham's faith was completed by his works.|Faith and works belong together. A faith that never acts is not saving faith.
james 3|Taming the tongue|Teachers will be judged more strictly. The tongue is small like a ship's rudder or a spark, yet it can set a forest on fire. With it we bless God and curse people made in his likeness. Wisdom from above is pure, peaceable and gentle.|Our words reveal our hearts. Controlling speech is a real test of maturity.
james 4|Humble yourselves|Fights come from desires at war inside us. Friendship with the world is enmity with God. Draw near to God and he will draw near to you. Do not boast about tomorrow; say, "If the Lord wills."|"God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble." Our plans are always in his hands.
james 5|Patience and the anointing of the sick|James warns the rich who cheat their workers. Be patient until the Lord comes, like a farmer waiting for rain. If anyone is sick, call the elders of the Church to pray over him and anoint him with oil. Confess your sins to one another.|This is the scriptural basis for the sacrament of the Anointing of the Sick. The prayer of a righteous person has great power.
1-peter 1|A living hope|Peter blesses God, who has given us new birth to a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus. Trials test faith as fire tests gold. Be holy, because God is holy. You were ransomed not with silver or gold but with the precious blood of Christ.|Our inheritance is kept safe in heaven. Holiness is the response to what it cost to save us.
1-peter 2|Living stones|Come to Christ, the living stone, and be built into a spiritual house. You are a chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation. Live honourably among unbelievers. When Christ suffered he did not threaten; by his wounds you have been healed.|All the baptised share in Christ's priesthood by offering their lives to God. Christ's patience is our example.
1-peter 3|Ready to give a reason|Peter advises wives and husbands, and tells everyone to be compassionate and not repay evil with evil. Always be ready to explain the hope that is in you, with gentleness and respect. Baptism now saves you, as the ark saved Noah.|This verse is the charter of apologetics: explain the faith, and do it kindly.
1-peter 4|Suffering as a Christian|Live the rest of your life for God's will. Love covers a multitude of sins. Use your gifts to serve one another. Do not be surprised at the fiery trial; rejoice to share Christ's sufferings.|Being insulted for the name of Christ is a blessing, not a disgrace.
1-peter 5|Shepherds and the roaring lion|Elders are to shepherd God's flock willingly, not for gain, as examples. Everyone should be clothed with humility. Cast all your anxiety on God, because he cares for you. Be watchful: the devil prowls like a roaring lion.|God cares about each worry we carry. The devil is real, and he is resisted by firm faith.
2-peter 1|Make your calling sure|God has given us everything needed for a godly life, so that we may share in the divine nature. Add virtue, knowledge, self-control and love to your faith. Peter was an eyewitness of the Transfiguration. No prophecy of Scripture is a matter of private interpretation.|Grace makes us sharers in God's own life. Scripture is to be read with the Church, not on our own authority.
2-peter 2|False teachers|False teachers will arise, bringing in destructive ideas and exploiting people out of greed. God judged the angels who sinned, the world of Noah and Sodom, and he knows how to rescue the godly. These teachers promise freedom while being slaves of corruption.|A promise of freedom that leads back into sin is a trap. God's judgement on evil is certain.
2-peter 3|The Lord is not slow|Scoffers ask where the promised coming is. With the Lord one day is as a thousand years. He is patient, not wanting anyone to perish. The day will come like a thief, and we look for new heavens and a new earth. Paul's letters contain things hard to understand, which some twist.|God's delay is mercy. Peter already ranks Paul's letters with "the other Scriptures".
1-john 1|God is light|John writes about what the apostles heard, saw and touched: the Word of life. God is light, and in him is no darkness. If we say we have no sin, we deceive ourselves; if we confess our sins, he is faithful and will forgive us.|The faith rests on eyewitnesses. Honesty about sin is the way to forgiveness.
1-john 2|Keep his commandments|If anyone sins, we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ. Whoever says he knows God but does not keep his commandments is a liar. Do not love the world. Antichrists have gone out from among us, those who deny that Jesus is the Christ.|Knowing God is shown by obeying him. Truth about who Jesus is cannot be traded away.
1-john 3|Children of God|See what love the Father has given us, that we should be called children of God. What we shall be has not yet appeared, but we shall be like him. Whoever hates his brother is a murderer. Let us love not in word but in deed and truth.|We are God's children now, and more is coming. Love is proved by action.
1-john 4|God is love|Test the spirits: every spirit that confesses Jesus Christ come in the flesh is from God. Love is from God, because God is love. He loved us first and sent his Son. Perfect love drives out fear. Whoever does not love a brother he can see cannot love God whom he cannot see.|"God is love" is the shortest summary of the faith. Love of God and love of neighbour cannot be separated.
1-john 5|Faith overcomes the world|Everyone who believes that Jesus is the Christ is born of God. This is the love of God, that we keep his commandments, and they are not burdensome. God gave us eternal life, and this life is in his Son. There is sin that leads to death and sin that does not.|John's distinction is one basis for the Church's teaching on mortal and venial sin. Eternal life begins now, in Christ.
2-john 1|Truth and love|The elder writes to "the chosen lady and her children", probably a local church. He rejoices that they walk in the truth and repeats the command to love one another. He warns against deceivers who deny that Jesus Christ came in the flesh.|Love and truth go together. Kindness does not mean welcoming false teaching about Christ.
3-john 1|Hospitality|The elder praises Gaius for welcoming travelling missionaries. He criticises Diotrephes, who loves to be first and refuses them. He commends Demetrius.|Supporting those who preach makes us co-workers with the truth. Ambition in the Church does real harm.
jude 1|Contend for the faith|Jude urges believers to fight for the faith handed on once for all to the saints. Certain people have crept in who turn grace into an excuse for immorality. He recalls God's past judgements and tells his readers to build themselves up, pray, and show mercy to those who doubt.|The faith is a gift entrusted to the Church, to be kept whole. Jude ends with a hymn to the God who can keep us from falling.
revelation 1|John's vision of the risen Christ|On the island of Patmos, on the Lord's day, John hears a voice like a trumpet and sees one like a son of man among seven golden lampstands, his face shining like the sun. He says, "Fear not. I am the first and the last, and the living one."|The book opens with Christ alive and in the midst of his churches. Its purpose is to give courage.
revelation 2|Letters to four churches|Christ speaks to Ephesus, which has lost its first love; to Smyrna, poor and persecuted; to Pergamum, which tolerates false teaching; and to Thyatira, which tolerates a false prophetess. Each letter ends with a promise to the one who conquers.|Christ knows each community exactly, its good and its bad. He corrects in order to save.
revelation 3|Letters to three more churches|Sardis has a reputation for being alive but is dead. Philadelphia has little power but has kept his word. Laodicea is lukewarm, neither hot nor cold. "Behold, I stand at the door and knock."|Comfort can be more dangerous than persecution. Christ knocks; he waits to be let in.
revelation 4|The throne in heaven|A door opens in heaven. John sees a throne with a rainbow around it, twenty-four elders, and four living creatures who never stop singing, "Holy, holy, holy, Lord God Almighty."|Before any trouble is shown, John sees who is in charge. The Sanctus at Mass echoes this song.
revelation 5|The Lamb and the scroll|No one is worthy to open the sealed scroll in God's hand, and John weeps. Then he sees a Lamb standing as though slain. The Lamb takes the scroll, and all heaven worships him.|History is in the hands of the crucified and risen Christ. The Lamb receives the same worship as God.
revelation 6|Six seals opened|The Lamb opens the seals. Four horsemen ride out, bringing conquest, war, famine and death. The martyrs under the altar cry, "How long?" The sixth seal shakes heaven and earth.|The evils of history are real, but they are limited and not the last word. God hears the martyrs.
revelation 7|The sealed and the countless crowd|God's servants are sealed, 144,000 from the tribes of Israel. Then John sees a multitude no one can count, from every nation, in white robes with palm branches. They have come through the great tribulation.|This is read on All Saints' Day. Heaven is full of people from everywhere, and God will wipe away every tear.
revelation 8|The seventh seal and the trumpets|There is silence in heaven. An angel offers incense with the prayers of the saints. Four trumpets sound, and a third of the earth, sea, rivers and sky are struck.|The prayers of God's people rise before him and matter in his plan.
revelation 9|Two more trumpets|The fifth trumpet releases locusts from the abyss that torment people for five months. The sixth releases a vast army. Yet the survivors do not repent of their idols or their crimes.|The plagues are warnings meant to turn people back. Hearts can stay hard even then.
revelation 10|The little scroll|A mighty angel gives John a small scroll to eat. It is sweet as honey in his mouth and bitter in his stomach. He is told he must prophesy again.|God's word is sweet to receive and hard to carry and proclaim.
revelation 11|Two witnesses and the seventh trumpet|Two witnesses prophesy, are killed by the beast, and are raised by God. The seventh trumpet sounds: "The kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord." The ark of the covenant is seen in heaven.|Faithful witness can look like defeat and end in victory.
revelation 12|The woman and the dragon|A woman clothed with the sun, crowned with twelve stars, gives birth to a son who will rule the nations. A great red dragon tries to devour him. Michael and his angels throw the dragon down. The dragon then makes war on the woman's other children.|The Church sees in the woman both God's people and Mary, mother of the Messiah. Satan is already a defeated enemy.
revelation 13|The two beasts|A beast rises from the sea with power from the dragon, and the world worships it. A second beast makes people worship the first and marks them with its number, 666.|The beasts stand for political power that demands worship and the propaganda that serves it. Christians must refuse to adore any state or ruler.
revelation 14|The Lamb on Mount Zion|The Lamb stands with the 144,000, who sing a new song. Angels announce the eternal Gospel and the fall of Babylon. "Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord." The earth is harvested.|Those who stay faithful are with the Lamb. Their deeds follow them.
revelation 15|Seven last plagues prepared|Those who conquered the beast stand beside a sea of glass and sing the song of Moses and of the Lamb. Seven angels receive seven bowls of God's wrath.|As Israel sang after the Red Sea, the saved praise God's justice.
revelation 16|The seven bowls|The bowls are poured out: sores, seas and rivers turned to blood, scorching heat, darkness, the Euphrates dried up, and a great earthquake. The kings gather at Armageddon. People curse God instead of repenting.|God's judgements are just. Evil finally destroys itself.
revelation 17|Babylon the great|An angel shows John a woman dressed in purple and scarlet, seated on a beast, drunk with the blood of the saints. She is the great city that rules the kings of the earth, and her own allies will turn on her.|For John's first readers this meant pagan Rome. It stands for every society built on luxury, idolatry and persecution.
revelation 18|Babylon falls|An angel cries, "Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great!" Kings, merchants and sailors weep because no one buys their cargo any more. God's people are told, "Come out of her."|Wealth built on injustice does not last. Christians are not to share in its sins.
revelation 19|The wedding of the Lamb|Heaven shouts "Alleluia". The marriage of the Lamb has come, and his bride is ready. Then a rider on a white horse, called Faithful and True, the Word of God, defeats the beast and its armies.|"Blessed are those called to the supper of the Lamb" is said at every Mass. Christ wins.
revelation 20|The thousand years and the last judgement|Satan is bound for a thousand years, released for a last revolt, and thrown into the lake of fire. The dead, great and small, stand before a great white throne and are judged by what they have done.|The Church reads the thousand years as the present age of Christ's reign, not a future earthly kingdom. Every life will be judged.
revelation 21|A new heaven and a new earth|The holy city, the new Jerusalem, comes down from God like a bride. God will live with his people; death, mourning and pain will be no more. The city has twelve gates and needs no sun, because the Lamb is its lamp.|"Behold, I make all things new." The goal of history is God dwelling with us.
revelation 22|Come, Lord Jesus|The river of the water of life flows from the throne, and the tree of life bears fruit every month. God's servants will see his face. Jesus says, "I am coming soon." The Spirit and the Bride say, "Come."|The Bible that began in a garden ends in a garden city. Its last prayer is for Christ to come.
